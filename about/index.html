Who I am

Hi, I'm Yang Guang — a developer who enjoys building things that live on the web. Whether it's a web app, a small CLI tool, or an AI experiment, if it involves code I'm probably curious about it.

I come from a full-stack background. Over the years I've quietly shipped corporate sites, content platforms, and data products, often moving things forward through collaboration. What I really care about isn't just the code — it's how content flows, how ideas travel, and how AI can actually fit into the way teams work day to day.

I started with curiosity and a text editor. That curiosity has slowly grown into a quiet appreciation for clean architecture, thoughtful design, and making technology feel a bit more human.

What I do

I build full-stack applications, explore AI tooling, and write down what I learn along the way. These days I'm focused on making complex ideas easier to understand — through small tools, clear writing, and honest examples.

About this site

This site is a static, hand-crafted portal — plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no frameworks or build tools. It's meant to stay fast, light, and easy to host almost anywhere. The design language borrows from awesome-design-md, and leans on the spirit of Vercel's visual system.

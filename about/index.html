Who I am

Hi there! I'm a developer who loves building things that live on the internet. Whether it's a sleek web app, a command-line tool, or an AI-powered experiment — if it involves code, I'm probably interested. With a full-stack background, I've spent years quietly shipping corporate sites, content platforms, and data products, often driving projects forward through collaboration. What I care about isn't just the code — it's content efficiency, how ideas travel, and how AI can genuinely find its way into the way teams actually work.

I started my journey with curiosity and a text editor. Over the years, that's evolved into a passion for clean architecture, thoughtful design, and making technology more accessible.

What I do

I build full-stack applications, explore AI/ML tooling, and write about what I learn. Currently, I'm focused on creating tools and content that help others understand complex technical concepts.
